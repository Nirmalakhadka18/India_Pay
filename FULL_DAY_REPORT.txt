6. STATUTORY COMPLIANCE & CALCULATION AUDIT (PF, ESI, PT, TDS)
================================================================================
All statutory deduction calculations were audited and verified compliant with
active Indian labour laws and tax regulations:

1. EMPLOYEES' PROVIDENT FUND (EPFO & EPS ACT, 1952):
   - Employee Contribution: 12% of basic wages (statutory ceiling: INR 15,000).
   - Employer Contribution Split:
     * EPS (Pension Account 10): 8.33%, capped at INR 1,250/month.
     * EPF (PF Account 1): 3.67% + excess.
     * Account 2 (Admin Charges): 0.50% (minimum INR 500).
     * Account 21 (EDLI): 0.50% of EPF wages.
   - EPFO ECR v2.0 Text Export: 11-field format delimited by '#~#' with UAN validation.

2. EMPLOYEES' STATE INSURANCE (ESIC ACT, 1948):
   - Employee Share: 0.75% of earned gross wages.
   - Employer Share: 3.25% of earned gross wages.
   - Wage Ceiling: INR 21,000/month.
   - Section 2(9) Proviso: Continued coverage through mid-cycle wage increments.
   - Rounding: Rounded UP to the nearest rupee per ESIC regulations.

3. PROFESSIONAL TAX (PT MULTI-STATE SCHEDULES):
   - Karnataka: Nil for gross < INR 25,000; INR 200/mo (March-Jan), INR 300 in Feb.
   - Maharashtra: Gender-differentiated schedules with female exemption threshold.
   - Telangana & Andhra Pradesh: Tiered monthly slab schedules.
   - Delhi & Haryana: Verified Nil-PT jurisdictions.

4. INCOME TAX & TDS (SECTION 192):
   - FY 2026-27 Form 138 Quarterly TDS Returns.
   - Legacy Form 24Q (Q1-Q4) with Annexure II Section 16 Standard Deduction (INR 75,000).
   - ITNS 281 Challan validation (7-digit BSR, 5-digit Challan Serial).
