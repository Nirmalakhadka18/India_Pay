2. MULTI-ORGANIZATION / MULTI-TENANT ARCHITECTURE CONFIRMATION
================================================================================
IndiaPay is strictly architected and proven as a MULTI-ORGANIZATION (multi-tenant) 
SaaS platform:

1. Workspace Hierarchy & Partitioning:
   - Every legal company or business entity operates as an independent 'workspace' 
     record in the database.
   - All tenant-owned records (employees, salaries, attendance, payrollRuns, 
     payrollItems, workspaceCompliance, taxDeclarations, tdsChallans, auditLogs, 
     and apiKeys) contain a mandatory 'workspaceId' foreign key.

2. Access Control & Role Segregation:
   - Users authenticate globally and join organizations via the 'members' table.
   - Roles are workspace-scoped:
     * 'owner': Full control, payroll finalization, compliance setup, banking config.
     * 'admin': Employee management, attendance entry, payroll submission (Maker).
     * 'member': Self-service employee portal (My Portal), view own masked payslips.
   - Cross-Tenant Security: Over 59 security assertions in 'security.test.ts' and 
     the new 'twoOrganizationUat.test.ts' verify that an authenticated user in 
     Organization 1 cannot view, create, edit, or approve records in Organization 2. 
     Any cross-tenant IDOR access results in immediate HTTP 403 / unauthorized rejection.

7. COMPREHENSIVE STATUTORY FORMULAS & THRESHOLD REFERENCE
================================================================================
A. Employees' Provident Fund (EPF & EPS):
   - Mandatory Wage Ceiling: ₹15,000 per month.
   - Employee Contribution: 12% of EPF wages (capped at ₹1,800/month unless 
     voluntary 'epfOnActualBasic' is selected).
   - Employer Pension Scheme (EPS): 8.33% capped at ₹1,250/month. Redirected 
     to EPF if employee is 58 years or older.
   - Employer EPF: 3.67% (12% minus EPS share).
   - EDLI: 0.5% on wages up to ₹15,000 ceiling.
   - Admin Charges: 0.5% (Account 2) with establishment minimum ₹500/month check.

B. Employees' State Insurance (ESI):
   - Gross Wage Ceiling: ₹21,000 per month (₹25,000 for employees with disability).
   - Contribution Rates: 0.75% Employee share | 3.25% Employer contribution.
   - Section 2(9) Proviso: If an employee's wage exceeds ₹21,000 during a contribution 
     period (April–Sept or Oct–March), coverage continues until the end of that period.

C. Professional Tax (PT):
   - State-specific slab rules implemented dynamically:
     * Karnataka: ₹0 for gross < ₹25,000; ₹200 for gross >= ₹25,000.
     * Maharashtra: ₹0 (< ₹7,500), ₹175 (₹7,500–₹10,000), ₹200 (> ₹10,000; ₹300 in Feb).
     * West Bengal, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat also supported.

D. Labour Welfare Fund (LWF):
   - State-specific periodic deductions:
     * Maharashtra: ₹12 EE / ₹36 ER semi-annually (June & December).
     * Karnataka: ₹20 EE / ₹40 ER annually (December).

E. Income Tax (TDS) under Section 115BAC (Finance Act 2024 / Act 2025):
