3. SALARY STRUCTURE & MONTHLY WAGE CALCULATION ENGINE
================================================================================
In response to the user's specific guidance regarding calculation of monthly wages:

3.1 BASIC SALARY & COMPONENT BREAKDOWN:
- Monthly Gross Salary = Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance (DA) + House Rent Allowance
  (HRA) + Conveyance Allowance + Special Allowance + Other Configured Allowances.
- CTC (Cost to Company) = Gross Earnings + Employer Statutory Contributions (EPF,
  EPS, EDLI, Admin, ESI, LWF) + Non-cash Benefits.

3.2 LOSS OF PAY (LOP) & PRORATION:
- Configurable Proration Basis: Supports calendar days in month, fixed 30 days, or
  actual working days.
- Daily Rate = Monthly Gross / Days in Month.
- LOP Deduction = Total Unpaid Leave Days * Daily Rate.
- Earned Gross Wages = Gross Salary - LOP Deduction.

3.3 STATUTORY WAGE BASES DETERMINATION:
- Universal salary base is NOT applied to every contribution:
  * EPF Eligible Wage: Basic Salary + DA (capped at ₹15,000 statutory ceiling unless
    voluntary full basic is chosen).
  * ESI Wages: Total Earned Gross Wages (Basic + DA + HRA + Special Allowance + all
    cash allowances). Threshold is ₹21,000/month gross.
  * Professional Tax Wage: Earned Gross Salary subject to state-specific slab exemptions.
  * TDS (Income Tax): Taxable earnings under Old Regime (with exemptions) or New Regime
    (Section 115BAC default with standard deduction).
