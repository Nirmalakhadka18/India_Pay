3. STATUTORY COMPLIANCE & PAYROLL CALCULATION AUDIT
================================================================================
A comprehensive verification of all statutory calculations was conducted against
active Indian labour laws and tax regulations:

1. EMPLOYEES' STATE INSURANCE (ESIC ACT, 1948):
   - Employee Contribution: 0.75% of earned gross wages.
   - Employer Contribution: 3.25% of earned gross wages.
   - Wage Ceiling: INR 21,000 per month.
   - Mid-Contribution Period Coverage: Proviso to Section 2(9) verified — employees
     crossing INR 21,000 mid-period remain covered through the contribution cycle.
   - Rounding Rule: Rounded UP to the next whole rupee per ESIC regulations.

2. EMPLOYEES' PROVIDENT FUND (EPFO & EPS ACT, 1952):
   - Employee Share: 12% of basic wages (statutory ceiling: INR 15,000).
   - Employer Share Split:
     * 8.33% to EPS (Pension Account 10), capped strictly at INR 1,250.
     * 3.67% to EPF (PF Account 1), plus remainder.
   - Administrative Charges:
     * Account 2 (Admin Charges): 0.50% with statutory minimum of INR 500.
     * Account 21 (EDLI): 0.50% of EPF wages.
   - EPFO ECR v2.0 Export: Delimited with exact '#~#' delimiter, 11 fields,
     sanitized member names, and verified 12-digit numeric UANs.

3. PROFESSIONAL TAX (PT MULTI-STATE SCHEDULES):
   - Karnataka: Nil for gross < INR 25,000; INR 200/mo (March-Jan), INR 300 in Feb.
   - Maharashtra: Gender-differentiated schedules (female exemption threshold).
   - Telangana & Andhra Pradesh: Tiered salary slab schedules.
   - Delhi & Haryana: Verified as Nil-PT jurisdictions without default fallback.

4. INCOME TAX & TDS (SECTION 192):
   - FY 2026-27: Implemented Form 138 Quarterly TDS Returns.
   - FY 2025-26 & Legacy: Form 24Q (Q1-Q4) with Annexure II Section 16 Standard
     Deduction (INR 75,000) and Chapter VI-A deductions.
   - ITNS 281 Challan Management: 7-digit BSR code and 5-digit serial validation.

5. PAYSLIP STATUS LIFECYCLE:
   - Calculated: Run generated in draft preview; payslips not yet visible to staff.
   - Approved: Run reviewed and authorized by an independent administrator.
   - Processed / Finalized: Run committed and locked; payslips are published and
     available for employee PDF generation and download.
